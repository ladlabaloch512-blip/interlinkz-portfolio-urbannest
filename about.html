About Urban Nest Realty

The agency we wanted to use ourselves: honest prices, full facts and no pressure.

Why we started

Urban Nest Realty started with one simple frustration: property listings that waste your time. Wrong prices, old photos, homes that were never really available. So we built the agency we wanted to use. Every listing on this site is visited and checked by our own team before it goes live, the price you see is the price the owner has agreed, and the facts are written the way we found them, good and bad. We help people buy, rent and sell across the city, and we would rather lose a deal than push a home that does not fit. That is the whole business, really.

How we verify listings

Our team visits every property before it is listed, checks the facts and takes note of the condition. If something changes, the listing is updated or removed. The price you see is the price the owner has agreed with us, and where there is room for discussion on a sale, we will tell you honestly before you make an offer.

Honest prices

The price shown is the owner’s asking price agreed with us. No bait prices, no silent mark ups.

Facts as we found them

Beds, baths, sizes and features are written the way our team found them on the visit, good and bad.

No pressure

Book a viewing when you are ready. Just looking is fine too, and the area guides are free for everyone.
