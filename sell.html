Sell With Us

An honest agency for your sale: checked facts, proper photos, viewings handled for you, and one clear fee that you only pay when the sale completes.

How selling works

1

Agree the price

We visit your home, check its facts and agree an honest asking price with you. No inflated promises to win the listing.

2

We list and show it

Your home goes live with checked facts and proper photos. We handle the enquiries and meet buyers at viewings.

3

Complete, with costs explained

When a buyer is found, we handle the paperwork steps with you and explain every cost before you sign anything.

What it costs

Agency fee on a completed sale: 1 percent of the sale price, paid only when the sale completes. No fee for listing, no fee for viewings, no charge if we do not sell it.

That is the whole fee. There are no add ons for photos, listings or advice.
