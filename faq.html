Buying steps, paperwork costs, viewing bookings, rental deposits and selling fees, answered plainly.

The price shown is the owner’s asking price agreed with us. For sale properties there may be a small room for discussion, and we will tell you honestly where that room is before you make an offer.

On top of the price, buyers should budget for transfer and registration charges, stamp duty and legal fees. The exact figures depend on the property value and area, and we give you a written estimate before you commit to anything.

Use the enquiry form on any property page, or call us. We confirm a time the same day where we can, and a member of our team meets you at the property.

Most landlords ask for 2 months rent as a security deposit plus the first month in advance. The listing page and our team will confirm the exact terms for the home you choose before you sign.
